## Crew Arrival & Power-Up

1. Crew Boards Aircraft — The flight crew enters via the terminal jetway, walks down the service stairs to the tarmac, and climbs up the 737's airstairs/mobile stairs to enter the unpowered cockpit.
2. Power on plane (Battery & GPU) — The pilot switches on the battery, and then connects the Ground Power Unit (GPU) that was already plugged into the aircraft belly by ground crew.
3. Connect jetway — Now that the aircraft has full power, the gate agent drives the jetway up to the open L1 door.
4. Preflight procedures (FMC & Checklist) — [OVERLAP START] Started immediately. The pilot aligns the IRS (takes 5–10 minutes) and begins programming the flight plan, performance numbers, and routing into the FMC.

## Ground Servicing & Clearance

5. Refueling — [OVERLAP] Fuel trucks pump fuel according to the flight plan while the pilot works on the FMC.
6. Board luggage / Cargo — [OVERLAP] Baggage handlers load the cargo pits simultaneously with refueling.
7. Request IFR clearance — The pilot contacts ATC to receive the IFR routing and departure instructions, allowing them to finalize the departure pages in the FMC.

## Boarding & Final Cockpit Prep

8. Board passengers — [OVERLAP] Passengers begin walking through the jetway. Note: The wheel chocks must remain firmly in place during this entire process to prevent the plane from shifting against the jetway.
9. Powering on APU — Done roughly 10–15 minutes before the scheduled departure time.
10. Disconnecting GPU — The pilot transfers the aircraft’s electrical load to the APU generators, and the ground crew disconnects the GPU cable from the belly.
11. Connecting pushback tug — [OVERLAP] While passenger boarding wraps up, the ground crew drives the pushback tug into position and connects it to the nose gear (but they do not touch the chocks yet).

## Cabin Closure & Departure

12. Closing passenger doors — Once the gate agent delivers the final passenger manifest, the cabin doors and cargo doors are closed and locked.
13. Disconnecting jetway — The jetway is fully retracted away from the fuselage, eliminating any risk of structural damage.
14. Arming passenger doors — Flight attendants put the doors into "Automatic" (or Flight Detent) so the emergency slides will deploy if a door is opened.
15. Removing wheel chocks — With the jetway safely gone and the tug confirmed secure, the ground crew pulls the wheel chocks away from the tires.
16. Getting push and start clearance — The pilot requests pushback and engine start clearance from ATC.
17. Push and start — The parking brake is released, the tug begins pushing the 737 backward into the taxiway, and the pilot initiates the engine start sequence.
